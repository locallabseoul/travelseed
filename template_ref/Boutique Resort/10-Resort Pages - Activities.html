Important Information

Booking & Reservations

We recommend booking activities at least 24 hours in advance to secure your preferred time slot. Some popular experiences may require earlier booking during peak season.

  • Book through concierge, guest portal, or WhatsApp
  • Confirmation email sent within 2 hours
  • Free cancellation up to 12 hours before start time

Age & Health Requirements

Certain activities have age restrictions and health requirements for safety. Please review requirements before booking.

  • Water activities: Minimum age 8 years (with adult)
  • Scuba diving: Medical clearance required
  • Hiking: Good physical condition recommended

Weather Policy

Outdoor activities are subject to weather conditions. In case of unfavorable weather, we will offer rescheduling or full refund. Our team monitors conditions closely to ensure guest safety.
