Important Information

Booking Requirements: All promotional packages must be booked directly through our official website or reservation team. Third-party bookings are not eligible for campaign benefits.

Payment Policy: Full prepayment required at time of booking for promotional rates. Standard cancellation policies apply unless otherwise specified in the offer details.

Validity: Offers are valid for new bookings only and cannot be combined with other promotions, discounts, or loyalty rewards unless explicitly stated.

Modifications: Date changes subject to availability and may require rate adjustment. Package components are non-transferable and non-refundable.

Age Restrictions: Some packages may have minimum age requirements. Please verify eligibility before booking.

Peak Season Exclusions (All Packages):

  • December 20 - January 5 (Holiday Season)
  • February 10-20 (Valentine's Week)
  • July 1-15 (Summer Peak)
  • All public holidays and long weekends

Package-Specific Blackouts:

  • Honeymoon Package: Not available during school holidays
  • Family Package: Limited availability during conference season (March, September)
  • Wellness Retreat: Blackout during resort maintenance (January 15-31)

* Blackout dates are subject to change. Please verify availability at time of booking.

Standard Packages (Honeymoon, Family, Wellness):

  • Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival - full refund
  • 7-14 days before arrival - 50% refund
  • Less than 7 days - non-refundable

Premium Packages (Ocean Explorer, Gourmet Experience):

  • Free cancellation up to 21 days before arrival
  • 14-21 days - 25% cancellation fee
  • Less than 14 days - non-refundable

All cancellations must be submitted in writing via email. Refunds processed within 7-10 business days.

Check-in/Check-out: Standard times apply (3 PM check-in, 11 AM check-out). Early check-in/late check-out subject to availability and may incur additional charges.

Inclusions: All package inclusions must be used during the stay and cannot be exchanged for cash value or transferred to future bookings.

Gratuities: Service charges and taxes are included in package rates. Additional gratuities are at guest's discretion.

Weather: Outdoor activities are weather-dependent. Alternative indoor experiences will be offered in case of inclement weather.

Special Requests: Dietary requirements, accessibility needs, and special occasions should be communicated at least 72 hours before arrival.
