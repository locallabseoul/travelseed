The Horizon Restaurant

Experience the perfect harmony of contemporary cuisine and traditional coastal flavors. Our award-winning restaurant showcases the bounty of the ocean and local farms through innovative dishes that celebrate seasonality and sustainability.

Set against panoramic ocean views, The Horizon offers an intimate dining atmosphere where every meal becomes a memorable occasion. Our open kitchen design allows guests to witness the artistry of our culinary team as they prepare each dish with precision and passion.

Culinary Concept

Farm-to-table philosophy meets coastal refinement. We partner with local fishermen, organic farmers, and artisan producers to bring you the freshest ingredients, transformed into contemporary dishes that honor tradition while embracing innovation.
