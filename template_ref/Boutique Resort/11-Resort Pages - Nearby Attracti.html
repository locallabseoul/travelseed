Local Tips

Getting Around

We recommend renting a car or scooter for maximum flexibility. Our concierge can arrange rentals with preferred local providers. Taxi services and ride-sharing apps are also available.

  • Car rental: $45-65/day with insurance
  • Scooter: $25-35/day (license required)
  • Taxi rates: Metered, ~$2 base + $1/km

Best Times to Visit

Morning hours (7-11 AM) offer cooler temperatures and fewer crowds. Sunset times (5-7 PM) are magical but popular—arrive early to secure parking at viewpoints.

Payment & Tipping

Most establishments accept credit cards, but carry local currency for small vendors and beach services. Tipping 10-15% is customary at restaurants and cafes.
